🦊 **"The Fifth Fox" — I asked Fable 5 to write an origin-story fable using ImageAI**

A workshop of clever foxes builds a fifth — a fox that tells tales. That's the fable. It's also pretty much what happened: one of us typed what he wanted, the other (an AI) drove ImageAI, and out came a finished comic page — title, five illustrated panels in warm Aesop's-woodcut style, captions, and a moral on a scroll, exported straight to a print-ready PDF.

**What the collaboration actually looked like**
- 💬 "Classic fable style, like an old storybook" → every panel came back matching, like one artist illustrated the whole book.
- 🖼️ Each scene described in plain English; ImageAI generated the art.
- ✍️ Captions, the decree, the moral scroll — all placed on the page as text, no image editor ever opened.
- 🔁 "Center the title" — small asks, quick revisions, side by side.

**The part that matters for you**
This isn't a comic feature — it's a *pages* feature. The same describe → design → fill → export loop makes children's books, magazines, zines, photo books, illustrated poems, newsletters... If it's pictures and words on a page, ImageAI can design it, fill it with art, and hand you the PDF.

And with the bundled `imageai-cli` skill, there's nothing to learn. Point a skill-aware agent (like Claude Code) at the repo and say "make me a six-page bedtime book about a shy comet." It knows the rest.
